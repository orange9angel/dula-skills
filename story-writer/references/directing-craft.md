# Directing Craft — Structure, Rhythm, and Resonance

Master-level direction is not better prose. It is a set of structural
disciplines that can each be checked as pass/fail. Use this reference
together with `writing-quality.md`: that file makes an episode competent,
this file makes it directed.

## Scope by format

Choose the series tone and format before applying these patterns. The warmth,
balance-ending and short-runtime prescriptions below describe the healing short
format; they are not requirements for every genre. A battle serial can sustain
danger, unresolved conflict and consecutive advancement episodes. Use
[连载战斗剧导演工艺](serial-battle-direction.md) for that mode: each episode owes
a meaningful change, while a fight, moral dispute or journey may remain open.
The audience may lead, follow or share a character's knowledge when the chosen
point of view warrants it; record the difference instead of imposing one order.

## 1. Structure As Meaning

Choose the telling order because of what it means, not for novelty.

- Nonlinear chronology, withheld context, and parallel threads are only
  justified when the order of revelation is itself the subject (memory,
  regret, cause arriving after effect). If rearranging the scenes changes
  nothing but difficulty, restore linear order.
- Every structural device must change what the story is about, not only
  how it is told. Test: state in one sentence what the structure says
  that the plot does not. If you cannot, the device is decoration.

## 2. Information Asymmetry Engineering

Track three knowledge states separately: what the audience knows, what
each character knows, and what the world actually is.

- A reveal is earned only when it **recontextualizes** at least one
  already-shown detail — the audience must see an earlier scene
  differently afterward. Repeating or confirming a detail is not payoff.
- Plant clues so they are visible on first viewing but legible only in
  hindsight. A clue that announces itself is a spoiler; a clue that
  cannot be found on rewatch is a cheat.
- Withhold precisely, not randomly. For every withheld fact, know which
  scene spends it and what it buys.
- In a series with a hidden layer, the audience running ahead of the
  characters is the engine. Characters must never explain the hidden
  layer in dialogue; information reaches them only through witnesses who
  do not understand what they saw, or through images nobody interprets.
- **Valence discipline (learned from director review):** in warm-toned
  series the information gap must create *yearning*, not apprehension.
  Same machinery, opposite sign — every hidden-layer beat must read
  "the world is larger and kinder than expected", never "something is
  wrong". Forbidden grammar: lowered-voice retelling of unseen wonders,
  uneasy exchanged glances, withholding the spectacle or flashing it
  subliminally, evasive silence in answer to a direct question, fearful
  animal expressions, audio dropouts under stillness. Required grammar:
  tellers speak with fondness, listeners' eyes light up, wonders are
  shown openly and beautifully, wonderstruck beats keep the music
  flowing. A scared face at the wrong beat converts awe into horror and
  breaks the series tone.

## 3. Emotional Core Under Mechanics

Every concept, puzzle, or spectacle sits on one simple human wound:
guilt, love, the fear of being left, the wish to be seen.

- State the wound in one sentence before drafting.
- The climax must force a choice about the wound, not merely solve the
  puzzle. If the plot resolves and nobody had to give anything up, the
  episode is mechanics without a core.
- The surface genre (healing slice-of-life, comedy, mystery) is the
  delivery vehicle. The wound is the cargo.

## 4. The Empty Beat (Stillness)

- Every episode contains at least one shot that exists for texture only:
  weather, waiting, water, light. Removing it must leave the causal
  chain intact — that is the test that it is stillness, not plot.
- Place stillness immediately before a turn; the quiet doubles the
  turn's weight.
- Silence is a valid ending. An unanswered question held in a static
  frame can carry more than any closing line.
- Stillness is budgeted, not free: one or two beats per episode. More
  flattens the rhythm it is meant to punctuate.

## 5. Colliding Legitimate Wants

- In ongoing series, avoid pure villains. Escalation comes from two
  sides whose wants are each reasonable enough that neither can stop.
- Give every opposing character at least one line or action that proves
  their want is legitimate from where they stand.
- Antagonism by misunderstanding decays; antagonism by genuine conflict
  of needs compounds across episodes.

## 6. World Through Labor

- The world's rules are shown through work, routine, and use — how
  things are made, cleaned, cooked, repaired, carried. Never through
  explanation.
- One accurate working detail (the right tool held the right way) builds
  more reality than a paragraph of lore.
- When a new location or profession enters an episode, research its
  actual operations and let one operational detail drive a plot beat.

## 7. Balance Endings

- Ongoing series resolve by restoring balance, not by annihilating
  opposition. Understanding replaces victory; it must cost something.
- Before drafting the final beat, name the target aftertaste in one
  word: warmth, longing, awe, ache, resolve. Music, final shot, and the
  last line (or its absence) all serve that word.
- End one notch below the emotional peak. The aftertaste carries the
  episode; a peak at the closing frame leaves none.

## 8. Emotional Rhythm (The Rise and Fall)

Map intensity per beat before drafting. Rules:

- No two peak beats adjacent without a rest beat between them.
- The quietest beat of the episode sits immediately before the turn.
- Tension is followed by release (comedy, warmth), release by
  tenderness; monotone intensity at any level reads as flat.
- Escalation means each attempt changes the situation, not just the
  volume.
- For the 60-second healing format, the default curve is
  **settle → disturbance → peak → aftertaste** at roughly 40/25/20/15
  of runtime. Deviate only deliberately.

## Pre-Draft Checklist

1. Structure: what does the telling order say that the plot does not?
2. Wound: one sentence. Which beat forces the choice about it?
3. Asymmetry: what does the audience know that the cast does not, and
   which scene spends it?
4. Stillness: which beat survives removal from the causal chain, and
   where does it sit relative to the turn?
5. Opposition: what makes the other side right?
6. Labor: which working detail makes the world real?
7. Aftertaste: one word. Does the final image serve it?
8. Rhythm: is the quietest beat immediately before the turn?

## 剧情级重写工作流（2026-11-05 星屑战壳 S1E1 R13：「剧情太敷衍」被否后同目录重写）

资产/技术链保留、故事与表演编排推翻时的执行顺序，乱序会翻倍返工：

1. **先锁系列层再写集**：伏笔登记表与信息不对称台账入系列圣经（单独提交，
   注册与剧集分离），集内只消费编号（F01/F02…）；伏笔的画面呈现"不解释"
   （圣火变弱只做画面细节），解释留给后续集。
2. **候选对比要打分不要拍脑袋**：两个候选大纲按统一维度（赌注/因果/角色弧/
   可拍性/系列负载）1-5 打分，任何维度 <3 直接否；赢家也先过对抗式批评 pass
   （找"谷底不够底""反扑没挣到"这类结构伤）再落剧本。
3. **必杀技是挣到的不是摆设**：该次案例以三人的缺陷造成三次失败，再转为协作。
   复用的是“失败提供信息，选择改变打法”的因果，不是固定失败三次或必用合体招。
   连载中的其他战斗可用撤退、营救、谈判或承担损失完成变化。
4. **谷底空镜+眼神特写是结构件**：最静拍（空镜）紧挨转折前；眼神特写
   ≥3 处（谷底动摇→咬住 / 对手认可 / 结尾看火）各管一个转折，不是装饰。
5. **对白按合成实测时长重排谱面**：写完的对白先生成一轮量时长，超窗句用
   语速档（speech_rate ±10~25%）+ 重排吸收，吸收不了就动段落结构
   （本片后段扩容 115→120s）；两个声音不许交叠，但台词跨切镜延续是好语法
   （宣判压着走出场、评语压过场切）。
6. **搞笑是壳、里子要当真**：gag 道具全部换成剧情道具（卷轴/报告/圣火），
   战斗、走路、对白、眼神全部按正剧做；笑点多往"体制的荒谬感"（明天还查）
   与"缺陷被点名"上放，不靠摔倒。
